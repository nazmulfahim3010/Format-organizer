# Impact of Remote Work on Productivity

## 1. Introduction

Remote work became mainstream after 2020. This report summarises **key findings** and *open questions*.

## 2. Methodology

We used a mixed-methods approach:

1. **Survey** of 1,200 employees
   - Online questionnaire
   - 25 questions
2. **Interviews** with 30 managers
3. Analysis of output metrics

### 2.1 Data sources

- HR records
- Project tracking tools (e.g., `Jira`)
- Self-reported time logs

---

## 3. Results

**Table 1:** Productivity change by role

| Role       | Change (%) | Sample |
|:-----------|-----------:|:------:|
| Engineer   | +12        | 540    |
| Designer   | +4         | 210    |
| Support    | -3         | 450    |

*Source: Company survey, 2024*

> Note that self-reported data may overstate gains.
